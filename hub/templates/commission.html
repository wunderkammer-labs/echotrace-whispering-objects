If Nothing Appears

If No Nodes Appear Yet

These are the most common first-install causes when the dashboard sees no objects.

  • The Raspberry Pis are still powered off or not on the same network as the hub.
  • broker_host in node_config.yaml does not match the hub IP address.
  • The MQTT broker is not running on the hub yet.

If Nothing Is Listed

If the Story List Is Empty

The hub can run only after at least one story pack has been copied into place.

  • Copy a story pack folder into content-packs/ on the hub.
  • Open Change Story and confirm the pack appears in the list.
  • Activate the story before naming objects or printing visitor cards.
