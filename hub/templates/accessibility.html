Use Order

How to Use This Page

Presets are the fastest path. Whole-gallery settings affect the room. Per-object settings override only one object.

  • Accessibility preset: best for hearing, vision, sensory, or mobility needs across the installation.
  • Operating preset: best for audience patterns such as school groups or quiet periods.
  • Per-object adjustment: use only when one object behaves differently from the rest.
